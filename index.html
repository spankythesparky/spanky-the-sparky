Section 04 · Why

Why we built this

01

Phone trees fail

When a hurricane hits at 2am, who's awake to answer the call? A central feed means linemen can opt in once and see every active call in their region.

02

Companies short-staff

Most storm shortages aren't from too few linemen. They're from contractors not knowing which travelers are available. We close that gap.

03

Free for everyone

No middlemen taking a cut of your hours. No subscription. No agency fees. Just a directory built by people who've been on the wrong side of a missed call.

04

Verified contractors

Every company on the directory has been vetted. No fake postings. No scams. Just IBEW signatory contractors who actually run storm rosters.
